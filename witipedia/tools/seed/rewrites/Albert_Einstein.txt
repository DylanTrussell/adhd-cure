'''Albert Einstein''' won the 1921 Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect, not for relativity. The committee did not consider relativity sufficiently confirmed, and the prize citation mentions only "his services to Theoretical Physics, and especially for his discovery of the law of the photoelectric effect."<ref name="nobel">Nobel Committee, announcement of the 1921 prize.</ref>

== The brain ==
Einstein died on 18 April 1955. The pathologist Thomas Harvey removed his brain during the autopsy without asking the family's permission, and kept it. Harvey later obtained retroactive consent from the son, Hans Albert, on condition that it be used only for science. He kept the brain in jars for about 40 years, cut into some 240 blocks, and mailed slices to researchers.<ref name="isaac">Walter Isaacson, ''Einstein: His Life and Universe'' (2007).</ref> In 1997 Harvey drove across the country with part of it in a Tupperware container to deliver it to Einstein's granddaughter, a trip told in Michael Paterniti's book ''Driving Mr. Albert''. She did not want it.<ref name="pat">Michael Paterniti, ''Driving Mr. Albert'' (2000).</ref>

== The tongue ==
The famous photograph of him sticking out his tongue was taken on 14 March 1951, his 72nd birthday, by the United Press photographer Arthur Sasse. Einstein liked the picture so much that he had nine prints made and used it on greeting cards.<ref name="isaac" />

== Math ==
He did not fail mathematics at school. He mastered calculus before he was 15 and in 1935, when told of the myth, replied that he had never failed in mathematics.<ref name="calaprice">Alice Calaprice, ''The Ultimate Quotable Einstein'' (2010).</ref>

== The refrigerator ==
In 1930 he and the physicist Leo Szilard received US Patent 1,781,541 for a refrigerator with no moving parts, designed after Einstein read about a family poisoned by leaking refrigerant gas.<ref name="patent">US Patent 1,781,541, granted 11 November 1930.</ref>

== Misattributed ==
"Insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results" has no source in Einstein's writings. Quote researchers have found no evidence that he said it.<ref name="calaprice" />

== President ==
In 1952 Israel's prime minister David Ben-Gurion offered him the presidency of Israel. He declined, saying he lacked the natural aptitude and the experience to deal with people properly.<ref name="isaac" />
