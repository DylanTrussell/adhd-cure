'''Queen Victoria''' reigned for 63 years and seven months, the longest of any British monarch until Elizabeth II passed her in 2015. She stood about 4 feet 11 inches tall and survived at least seven attempts on her life.<ref name="hibbert">Christopher Hibbert, ''Queen Victoria: A Personal History'' (2000).</ref>

== The attempts ==
The tally depends on who is counting and which of the gunmen had loaded pistols, but the standard list runs from 1840 to 1882. Edward Oxford fired two pistols at her carriage in 1840 while she was pregnant. In 1850 a retired army officer, Robert Pate, hit her on the head with a cane, leaving her with a black eye and a bruise. In 1882 Roderick Maclean fired at her at Windsor railway station, and Eton schoolboys beat him with umbrellas.<ref name="hibbert" /> She later wrote that it was "worth being shot at, to see how much one is loved."

== "We are not amused" ==
The line is the most famous thing she never provably said. No contemporary record places it in her mouth. Her granddaughter Princess Alice later said she had never heard her grandmother say it, and had asked her about it.<ref name="longford">Elizabeth Longford, ''Victoria R.I.'' (1964).</ref> Treat it as legend.

== The wedding dress ==
Victoria married Prince Albert in 1840 in white satin, and her choice is generally credited with making white the fashionable colour for Western wedding dresses.<ref name="rct">Royal Collection Trust, wedding dress notes.</ref>

== Forty years of black ==
Albert died on 14 December 1861. Victoria wore mourning black for the remaining 39 years of her life. She also had his shaving water brought to his room each morning for years.<ref name="hibbert" />

== Empress ==
Parliament made her Empress of India in 1876, which pleased her far more than it pleased most of the people she ruled.
