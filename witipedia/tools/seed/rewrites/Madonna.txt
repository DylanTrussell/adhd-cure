'''Madonna''', born Madonna Louise Ciccone in Bay City, Michigan, in 1958, is the best-selling female recording artist of all time, according to Guinness World Records.<ref name="gwr">Guinness World Records, "Best-selling female recording artist."</ref>

== $35 ==
She moved to New York City in 1978, and later said she arrived with $35 in her pocket. The number has been repeated in nearly every biography. It is her own claim, not independently documented.<ref name="tara">J. Randy Taraborrelli, ''Madonna: An Intimate Biography'' (2001).</ref>

== The Pepsi ad ==
In 1989 Pepsi announced a $5 million deal with Madonna and showed a commercial for her song "Like a Prayer" on 2 March. The next day MTV debuted the song's actual music video, which included burning crosses and a Black saint who comes to life. Religious groups threatened a boycott. Pepsi cancelled the ad campaign on 4 April, and she kept the money.<ref name="pepsi">Pepsi-Cola corporate announcement, April 1989; ''Time'', 1989.</ref>

== Live Aid ==
She performed at Live Aid in Philadelphia on 13 July 1985, a few weeks after nude photographs from her early years had been published in ''Penthouse'' and ''Playboy''. She kept her jacket on and, as widely reported, joked that she would not be taking anything off that day because it might be held against her in ten years.<ref name="liveaid">Live Aid broadcast, Philadelphia, 13 July 1985.</ref>

== The cone ==
The conical corset worn on the 1990 Blond Ambition tour was designed by Jean Paul Gaultier.<ref name="tara" />

== Evita ==
She won the Golden Globe for Best Actress in a Musical or Comedy for her 1996 role as Eva Perón in ''Evita'', after reportedly writing a letter to the director Alan Parker to argue for the part.
