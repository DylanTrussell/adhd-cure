'''''Game of Thrones''''' is an HBO series adapted from George R. R. Martin's novels ''A Song of Ice and Fire''. It ran for eight seasons and 73 episodes from 2011 to 2019, won 59 Emmy Awards, and was repeatedly named the most-pirated television show in the world.<ref name="emmy">Television Academy, Emmy Awards database.</ref>

== The cup ==
In "The Last of the Starks", the fourth episode of the final season, broadcast on 5 May 2019, a disposable coffee cup sat in front of Daenerys Targaryen in a medieval feast scene. HBO acknowledged the error, saying "the latte that appeared in the episode was a mistake" and that Daenerys had ordered an herbal tea, and the cup was digitally removed from later releases. Fans said it was the most memorable thing in the episode.<ref name="cup">HBO statement, May 2019.</ref>

== Dothraki ==
The linguist David J. Peterson created the Dothraki and Valyrian languages for the show after winning a language-creation competition HBO ran. Martin's books contained only a small number of Dothraki words.<ref name="peterson">David J. Peterson, ''The Art of Language Invention'' (2015).</ref>

== The petition ==
After the final season, a Change.org petition to remake it with "competent writers" gathered more than a million signatures. HBO did not remake it.<ref name="petition">Change.org petition, May 2019.</ref>

== The books ==
The first novel appeared in 1996. The fifth, ''A Dance with Dragons'', appeared in 2011. The sixth, ''The Winds of Winter'', was announced for publication and had not been published as of the last time this article was fact-checked.<ref name="martin">George R. R. Martin, publication history.</ref> The television series finished before the book it was based on.

== The throne ==
Martin has said the show's Iron Throne is too small and not how he pictured it in the books, where it is described as a huge heap of swords from defeated enemies, towering over the hall.<ref name="martin" />

== Khaleesi ==
After the show, the baby name "Khaleesi" rose sharply in United States birth records. Khaleesi is a title meaning queen in the show's Dothraki language, not a given name, and it entered US baby-name statistics after the show aired.
